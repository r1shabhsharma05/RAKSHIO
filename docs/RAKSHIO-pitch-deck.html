01 · Problem

The first 60 minutes decide everything — and panic wastes them

  • After UPI fraud, the victim panics. They don't know the amount, date, UTR — or that 1930 exists.
  • Reporting fast matters: 1930 is best in the first 4 hours; the portal complaint within 24 hours of the 1930 call; RBI zero liability if the bank is told within 3 working days.
  • While panicking, victims fall for a second scam — fake "recovery agents" who charge a fee and vanish.
  • Existing tools are screen-first forms. A scared, low-literacy user cannot fill a form.
02 · Users

Who it's for

Elderly smartphone users

Can talk, can't navigate forms. Large text, one action per screen.

First-time UPI users

Fraud is their first encounter with banking disputes.

Low digital literacy

Hindi/Hinglish voice in, plain-language steps out.

Families & caregivers

Caregiver Mode + Care Circle SOS — someone trusted helps.

Pilot target: 100 users, Mathura, Dec 2026–Jan 2027 (target to evaluate, not a guaranteed outcome).

03 · Product

One core flow, done by speaking

  • Boliye kya hua? — 1–2 minutes of free speech, Hindi/Hinglish. No login, no form.
  • Samajh raha hoon — deterministic extraction: amount, time, UTR, bank, fraud type. Nothing guessed.
  • Kya ye sahi hai? — the app reads it back; the card locks only when the victim confirms.
  • Golden Hour Clock — live countdowns to the 1930 / portal / bank deadlines; the action queue re-orders itself.
  • 1930 Call Coach — a 30-second script written from the confirmed facts.
04 · What makes it different

Five differentiators

Golden Hour Clock

Deadlines as data, with sources. Actions re-order as time runs out.

Ack Bridge + Portal Copy Deck

1930 ack number in, 24h countdown on, portal fields copy-ready.

Tamper-evident Evidence Chain

SHA-256 hash-chained vault. Edit a record and Verify turns red.

Smart Triage Router

Money lost → 1930/bank/portal. Attempted fraud → Chakshu (30 days).

Second-Scam Shield 2.0

Weighted red-flag rules + Truth Card. 50-message test set: 100%.

Zero-key, on-device

No backend, no keys, no server. Starts anywhere, works offline.

05 · Demo — what it does

Working prototype, today

  • Full voice loop + confirmation gate + incident card + timeline.
  • Evidence Vault, Ack Bridge, Triage, Shield, Bank Alert Kit, Care Circle, Proof Pack (PDF/ZIP + root-hash QR).
  • Judge Mode: one button runs the seeded ₹20,000 UPI scenario — no mic needed.
What it does NOT do: it never files a report, never guarantees recovery, never asks for or stores OTP / PIN / CVV.
06 · Launch plan

From prototype to pilot

  • Dec 2026 – Jan 2027: 100-user pilot, Mathura. Measure action, not money recovered: 1930 contacted within 1 hour, portal complaint within 24 hours, recovery agents recognised and refused.
  • Feb – Apr 2027: regional languages, Fraud Fire-Drill, top-15 bank playbooks.
  • From May 2027: partnerships with banks, cyber cells, and community organisations.

Main risk, stated plainly: false reassurance — a user thinking that talking to RAKSHIO equals having reported. The app says, on every screen, that it files nothing.
